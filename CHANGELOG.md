# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-07

First release.

- One click to arm, recording starts at the first sound, click again to save to Downloads.
- Saves 256 kbps AAC in an M4A file, named after the tab. Falls back to WebM where the browser can't encode AAC.
- Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, Opera, Vivaldi, and other Chromium browsers.
